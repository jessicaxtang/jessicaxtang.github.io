jessica tang

AI Alignment, Interpretability, Ethics.

▹ I'm deeply interested in researching and developing interpretable and aligned AI systems to help society.

▹ I'm studying Machine Intelligence in Engineering Science at the University of Toronto, supervised by Sheila McIlraith and Silviu Pitis at Vector Institute.

▹ Previously I interned at Microsoft Research leading a project on multi-agent interpretability and influence tracing, with Sharad Agarwal and Shraddha Barke.

▹ I founded illuminAI, a boundless community to creatively blend computer science and arts, increasing public engagement of AI ethics and societal impacts. We host ethics hackathons, film videos and publish articles.

▹ Outside of research, I sing, perform, and write songs!

Research & Writing

Research projects archive.

AI interpretability & alignment

  • Optimizing a Margin of Safety via Prompt Repair for Large Language Models.

    Jessica Tang, Silviu Pitis, Sheila McIlraith. Machine Ethics workshop at AAAI 2026.

    An interpretable prompt-repair framework uses span-level edits and safety margins to localize risky text, propose minimal fixes, and verify safer outputs without retraining.

applied machine learning

  • Rehabilitation Exercise Quality Assessment and Feedback Generation Using Large Language Models with Prompt Engineering.

    Jessica Tang, Ali Abedi, Tracey JF Colella, Shehroz Khan. ARIAL workshop at IJCAI 2025. [Code]

    Pose features plus optimized prompts let LLMs assess exercise quality and generate personalized rehab feedback.

  • Spatio-temporal Multimodal Wildfire Spread Prediction: Exploring Vision Transformers and Lightweight Models.

    Jessica Tang, Hibah Hussain, Natasha Yang (equal contribution). ECE324 Final Project 2025.

    FireNet predicts wildfire spread from satellite imagery and weather data, outperforming CNN baselines.

  • Deep Reinforcement Learning Controller for Indoor Farming.

    Jessica Tang. International Student Conference on Artificial Intelligence 2021. 🪷 Best presenter award.

    A DRL controller adapts indoor farming conditions to improve growth and resource efficiency.

ethics, philosophy, and media

  • Disputations on Compatibilism and the No Choice Principle.

    Jessica Tang. PHL244 Human Nature, 2024.

    Examines Van Inwagen's No Choice Principle and argues that compatibilism remains defensible.

  • Does Mass Media Enable Genocide?

    Jessica Tang. Harvard College Undergraduate Research Association, 2020.

    Shows how broadcast propaganda can drive conformity and escalation, using the Rwandan Genocide.

engineering systems

  • What's Your Groove? Beat Tracking in Music Signal Processing.

    Jessica Tang. ECE355 Independent Study Module, 2024.

    Surveys tempo and beat detection with MIR features and STFT energy methods, plus industry use cases.

  • Modular Can-Sized Satellite System with Active Attitude Control.

    T. Cai, K. Howard, B. Zhou, J. Tang, V. He, D. Liu. European Space Agency, International CanSat Competition, 2022. 🪷 Outstanding Paper Award. [ESA Article]

    Design and implementation of a modular CanSat with active attitude control for stable descent.
